# LeRobot SO-101: what to check by camera

A starting list. Each item should be confirmed against the official LeRobot SO-101 docs before the first release.

- **Servo IDs and order:** every STS3215 has the right ID for its joint, set before assembly. A wrong ID shows up as the wrong joint moving.
- **Gear ratio per joint:** leader and follower arms use different gear ratios on some joints. A mixed-up servo stalls or moves too fast.
- **Cable daisy chain:** each servo cable is fully seated, and none are pinched at a joint over its range of motion.
- **Power:** the correct supply for the arm's voltage, polarity right, and the board's LED on.
- **Horn alignment:** the servo horn is mounted at the middle position, so calibration has room in both directions.
- **Calibration poses:** the arm matches the pose the calibration script asks for at each step.
- **Gripper:** the jaw closes fully without the servo straining. A straining servo is warm and buzzes.
